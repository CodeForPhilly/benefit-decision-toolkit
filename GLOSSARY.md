# Benefit Decision Toolkit

Tools for building, previewing, and publishing benefit eligibility screeners.

## Language

**Screener**:
A form an author builds that asks people questions and tells them which benefits they may qualify for.
_Avoid_: Survey, questionnaire

**Published screener**:
A snapshot of a screener that people being screened can open, on its own or from a connected CRM.
_Avoid_: Live screener, public form

**Preview**:
The editor's view of a screener as a person being screened would see it, evaluated against the author's unpublished work.
_Avoid_: Test mode, sandbox

**Screening session**:
One person's pass through a screener, in Preview or on a published screener: their answers, the evaluations of those answers, and the latest results.
_Avoid_: Screening flow, form session, evaluation flow
